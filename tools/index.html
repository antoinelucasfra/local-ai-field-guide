A field guide to running language models on your own machine.

Most of what you can do with a hosted API, you can do on a workstation under your desk — privately, offline, and for less money than you'd expect. This book walks through the whole path: picking a model, running it well, fine-tuning it on your data, building agents and RAG on top, serving it to a team, and staying on the right side of the GDPR.

Written for researchers and engineers; no cloud account required.
